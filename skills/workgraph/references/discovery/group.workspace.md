# workspace

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Workspaces hold independent local planning and context collections in the registry.

**Use when:** You need to create or open a workspace, inspect its overview or manage portable state.

**Choose carefully:** Registry membership and open daemon handles differ from a workspace's durable planning metadata.

**Start with:** [workspace.list](../api/workspace.list.md), [workspace.overview](../api/workspace.overview.md), [workspace.create](../api/workspace.create.md).
