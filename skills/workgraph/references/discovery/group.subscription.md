# subscription

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Subscriptions retain explicit notification routing preferences.

**Use when:** A participant needs scoped notification subscriptions with guarded updates.

**Choose carefully:** Subscriptions select delivery; they neither acknowledge notifications nor execute background workers.

**Start with:** [subscription.list](../api/subscription.list.md), [subscription.get](../api/subscription.get.md), [subscription.put](../api/subscription.put.md).
