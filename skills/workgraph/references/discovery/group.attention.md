# attention

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Attention combines filtered exact-source events for a worker to inspect or wait on.

**Use when:** An idle worker needs a bounded wakeup view without polling acquisition writes.

**Choose carefully:** Attention reads acknowledge nothing; inbox acknowledgements and ownership changes remain explicit.

**Start with:** [attention.read](../api/attention.read.md), [attention.wait](../api/attention.wait.md).

`workgraph help attention` · [Focused reference](../attention.md)

| --- | --- | --- |
