# daemon

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Daemon administration controls the local service and complete registry exports.

**Use when:** You need service health, graceful shutdown or a portable complete-state operation.

**Choose carefully:** The daemon stores local state; the harness owns workers and external side effects.

**Start with:** [daemon.health](../api/daemon.health.md), [daemon.shutdown](../api/daemon.shutdown.md), [daemon.export_all](../api/daemon.export_all.md).
