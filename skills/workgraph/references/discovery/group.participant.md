# participant

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Participants combine registered identities with retained attributed workspace writers.

**Use when:** You need to find who actually participated, including writers absent from the actor catalog.

**Choose carefully:** Observed attribution is retained-history evidence, not current presence or process liveness.

**Start with:** [participant.list](../api/participant.list.md).
