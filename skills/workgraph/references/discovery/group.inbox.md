# inbox

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Inboxes deliver durable recipient notifications and track explicit acknowledgements.

**Use when:** A participant needs unread notifications or must acknowledge selected delivered entries.

**Choose carefully:** Reading does not acknowledge; request delivery, responsibility and resolution are separate transitions.

**Start with:** [inbox.read](../api/inbox.read.md), [inbox.wait](../api/inbox.wait.md), [inbox.ack](../api/inbox.ack.md).
