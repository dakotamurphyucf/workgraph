# registry

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Registry receipts recover the recorded outcome of registry-scoped mutations.

**Use when:** A workspace registration or other registry operation may have committed without a reply.

**Choose carefully:** Registry receipt identities are separate from planning and history receipt namespaces.
