# recovery

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Recovery records retain guarded ownership recovery outcomes.

**Use when:** You need the audit of named or path ownership recovery already performed.

**Choose carefully:** Use ticket.recovery for ticket-specific records; a recovery record cannot prove an external process stopped.

**Start with:** [recovery.list](../api/recovery.list.md), [recovery.get](../api/recovery.get.md).
