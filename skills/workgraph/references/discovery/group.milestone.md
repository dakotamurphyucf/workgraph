# milestone

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Milestones group project progress toward dated checkpoints.

**Use when:** A project needs dated delivery checkpoints or ticket placement within them.

**Choose carefully:** Milestone dates organize planning; they do not schedule worker processes.

**Start with:** [milestone.list](../api/milestone.list.md), [milestone.create](../api/milestone.create.md), [milestone.schedule](../api/milestone.schedule.md).
