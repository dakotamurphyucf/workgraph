# decision

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Decisions preserve versioned rationale and evidence as shared records.

**Use when:** A durable choice and its history must outlast a discussion thread.

**Choose carefully:** A decision records rationale; it does not itself approve a submission or complete work.

**Start with:** [decision.list](../api/decision.list.md), [decision.get](../api/decision.get.md), [decision.put](../api/decision.put.md).
