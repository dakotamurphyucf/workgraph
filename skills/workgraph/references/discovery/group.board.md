# board

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Boards organize independent discussion threads within a workspace.

**Use when:** Discussion needs a durable named grouping separate from ticket ownership.

**Choose carefully:** Boards group threads; they do not create accountable requests or change ticket status.

**Start with:** [board.list](../api/board.list.md), [board.get](../api/board.get.md), [board.put](../api/board.put.md).
