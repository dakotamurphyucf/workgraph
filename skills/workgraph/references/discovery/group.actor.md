# actor

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Actors catalog the cooperating people and agents attributed in a workspace.

**Use when:** You need a named identity or an inventory of its held work and requests.

**Choose carefully:** An actor is attribution metadata; a run is one invocation and neither proves a process is alive.

**Start with:** [actor.overview](../api/actor.overview.md), [actor.list](../api/actor.list.md), [actor.put](../api/actor.put.md).
