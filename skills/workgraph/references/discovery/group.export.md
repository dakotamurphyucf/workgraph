# export

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Export jobs record complete portable-state export progress and verification.

**Use when:** You need to inspect, retry, cancel or verify an export already requested.

**Choose carefully:** A job record is distinct from the exported artifact and from readable workspace summaries.

**Start with:** [export.list](../api/export.list.md), [export.get](../api/export.get.md), [export.verify](../api/export.verify.md).
