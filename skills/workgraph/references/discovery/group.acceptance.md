# acceptance

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Acceptance criteria turn policy requirements into exact attributed assertions.

**Use when:** A project or ticket needs named criteria whose satisfaction is recorded against a policy revision.

**Choose carefully:** Acceptance assertions satisfy criterion policy; review decisions evaluate a submitted output generation.

**Start with:** [acceptance.policy.effective](../api/acceptance.policy.effective.md), [acceptance.assert](../api/acceptance.assert.md), [acceptance.assertions](../api/acceptance.assertions.md).
