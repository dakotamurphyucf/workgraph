# handoff

Generated from `workgraph discover --catalog`. Do not edit this file.

Handoffs capture structured objectives, progress, blockers and next steps under ownership.

**Use when:** An owner must leave reliable recovery context for a later invocation.

**Choose carefully:** Handoffs are explicit authored context; ticket.resume builds a bounded view of recorded sources.

**Start with:** [handoff.get](../api/handoff.get.md), [handoff.set](../api/handoff.set.md), [handoff.history](../api/handoff.history.md).
